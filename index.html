Questions.

When does it ship?

The first production run ships in December 2026, within the United States. You will hear from us before your box leaves.

What is in it?

Lightly sparkling water, PeptiStrong® (the clinically studied plant-derived peptide, from fava beans) and electrolytes: sodium, potassium and magnesium. Sweetened with monk fruit and stevia, plus a little cane sugar. No artificial colors, flavors or sweeteners.

How many should I drink?

One can a day. That is the daily serving used in the clinical studies, and each can holds one serving.

Why is it a little cloudy?

The light haze is the peptide. It settles a little in the can, so roll it once, then open.

Is it a soda? A protein shake?

Neither. It is a lightly sparkling, low calorie drink built around one clinically studied ingredient, and it is not sweet the way a soda is.

How does the subscription work?

Pick a plan: 2 boxes every 4 weeks or every 3 weeks at $3.49 a can, or 1 box every 2 weeks at $3.69 a can. Subscriptions ship free. Skip or cancel any time before your next delivery ships. It renews until you cancel.

Does it need to be refrigerated?

No. Store it in a cool, dry place. It tastes best cold.
