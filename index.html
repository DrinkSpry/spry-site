Every way to take a peptide.

Peptides are the most talked-about thing in strength and recovery. Almost all of it happens in vials, sold by strangers, with no label anyone can check. People do it anyway. So we made a drink with a peptide that has been clinically studied in humans.

What's in the can.

2.4 g

The studied amount, in every can

Each can carries 2.4 g of hydrolyzed fava bean protein, the source of the peptides and the daily amount used in the human studies. That is why Spry exists.

Cloudy on purpose

A light haze from the fava bean protein, which settles a little in the can. Roll it once, then open.

Lightly sparkling. Low calorie.

Sweetened with monk fruit and stevia, plus a little cane sugar. It drinks closer to a dry citrus soda than a sports drink. Electrolytes include sodium, potassium and magnesium.

One peptide. Clinically studied.

Two placebo-controlled, peer-reviewed human studies of the fava bean ingredient in Spry, at 2.4 g a day, the amount in every can. Both are cited below.

*Placebo-controlled, peer-reviewed human studies of hydrolyzed fava bean protein, the ingredient in Spry, taken as capsules at 2.4 g a day, the amount in one can. Strength: 72 healthy adults aged 19 to 40 on a resistance-training program, one-rep max leg extension at day 28 (Mohan et al., BMJ Nutrition, Prevention & Health, 2025). Recovery: 30 healthy men aged 30 to 45, rate of strength recovery over the 72 hours after a workout designed to cause muscle soreness (Kerr et al., Nutrients, 2023). These results are for the ingredient, not for Spry itself, and we are testing that the can keeps the ingredient intact.

What is in it?

Lightly sparkling water, 2.4 g of hydrolyzed fava bean protein (the clinically studied ingredient) and electrolytes: sodium, potassium and magnesium. Sweetened with monk fruit and stevia, plus a little cane sugar. No artificial colors, flavors or sweeteners. Each flavor has its own ingredient list and Nutrition Facts, and both go on this page before we ship.

Why is it a little cloudy?

The light haze comes from the fava bean protein. It settles a little in the can, so roll it once, then open.

Is it a soda? A protein shake?

Neither. It is a lightly sparkling, low calorie drink built around one clinically studied ingredient, and it is not sweet the way a soda is.

Does it need to be refrigerated?

No. Store it in a cool, dry place. It tastes best cold.
