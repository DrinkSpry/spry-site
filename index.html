What is in it?

One peptide made from fava beans and clinically studied in humans, 2.4 g a can, with electrolytes, real fruit and lightly sparkling water. Natural sweeteners from monk fruit and stevia, and a little real sugar. No artificial colors, flavors or sweeteners. The full panel goes on the can and on this page before we ship.

Why is it cloudy?

Because the peptide is in there. It settles a little in the can. Roll it once, then open.

Neither. It is a lightly sparkling drink built around one studied peptide, and it is not sweet the way a soda is.

Serve it cold. It tastes better that way.
